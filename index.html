Competitions

Four distinct tracks — choose the one that best fits your work

Paper Presentation
Oral  ·  Days 3 & 4  ·  Main Auditorium

Present your original research to a panel of national judges and 500+ delegates. Selected papers receive an 8-minute slot followed by a 2-minute Q&A. This is the flagship academic competition of the conference.

Submit: Abstract (300 words) + Full manuscript as .doc / .docx — Introduction · Methods · Results · Conclusion
Presentation: 8 min talk + 2 min Q&A. Bring your slides on the day (PPT or PDF)
Eligibility: All registered delegates, faculty & PG students. Presenting author must be registered
Originality: Unpublished, not submitted elsewhere. Max 5 co-authors
Best Paper  ·  Runner-up  ·  Young Investigator Award
e-Poster Presentation
Display  ·  Day 3 Afternoon  ·  e-Poster Hall

Display your research, case reports, or quality improvement projects as an e-poster. Judges review each e-poster during the dedicated viewing session. Presenters give a 3-minute summary and answer questions.

Submit: Abstract (300 words) + e-Poster as .ppt / .pptx — 1 slide only, A0 portrait (33.1 × 46.8 in)
Judging session: 3-min summary to judges, Day 3 13:00–14:00
Content: Title · Authors · Background · Methods · Results · Conclusions · Institution logo
Best e-Poster Award  ·  Runner-up e-Poster Award
CPC Competition
Clinical Problem Solving  ·  Day 4  ·  Morning

The Clinico-Pathological Case competition tests diagnostic reasoning and clinical acumen. Teams present a real-world EM case — history, investigations, differentials — and defend their final diagnosis before expert judges.

Submit: Case summary (300 words) + Slides as .ppt / .pptx — Case History → Investigations → Differentials → Diagnosis → Learning Points
Team: 2–3 members, all registered delegates or PG students
Presentation: 10 min + 5 min cross-examination by judges
Anonymise: All patient details de-identified. Do not reveal final diagnosis in submitted file
Best CPC Team  ·  Runner-up CPC Award
EM Quiz
Teams of 3  ·  Prelims + Finals  ·  Day 4 Morning

A high-energy emergency medicine quiz testing clinical knowledge, ECG & imaging interpretation, and rapid-fire recall. Prelims are written MCQs; top 6 teams compete in a live multi-round final with the audience watching.

Team: Exactly 2 members. Both must be registered delegates or PG students
Prelims: 30 MCQs in 30 minutes. Top 6 teams advance to live finals
Finals: 6 rounds — Direct · Visual (ECG/X-ray) · Rapid Fire · Lifeline · Audio-Visual · Sudden Death
Registration: No abstract submission needed — register as a quiz team during conference registration
Best Quiz Team  ·  Runner-up Quiz Team

General Rules
  • 01.Original research only — case reports, observational studies, audits, and clinical trials accepted.
  • 02.Work must not have been published or presented at any prior national or international conference.
  • 03.Presenting author must be a registered delegate at EM Karnataka 2026.
  • 04.Maximum 5 co-authors. All names must appear exactly as they should on the certificate.
  • 05.Presentation language is English. Local language labels on images permitted where clinically relevant.
  • 06.Strict time limit: 8 min talk, 2 min Q&A. Warning bell at 7 min. Presentation cut off at 8 min.
  • 07.Slides submitted to AV desk at least 1 hour before session. Format: PPT or PDF only.

Format & General Rules
  • 01.Teams of 3. All members must be registered delegates.
  • 02.Prelims: Written MCQ paper — 30 questions in 30 minutes. Top 6 teams qualify for live finals.
  • 03.Finals: 6 rounds — Direct, Visual, Rapid Fire, Lifeline, Audio-Visual, and Sudden Death.
  • 04.Direct questions: 10 pts. Passed questions open to other teams for 5 pts. No negative marking on passed questions.
  • 05.Rapid fire: 60 seconds per team, 5 pts per correct answer, no penalty for wrong answers.
  • 06.Each team gets one 30-second lifeline consult per finals. Must be used before answering.
  • 07.Quiz master's decision is final. No electronic devices during the quiz.
  • 08.Register as a team of 3 during conference registration.

Submission File Format Instructions

Read carefully before uploading — incorrect formats will be returned for revision

Paper Manuscript
Oral presentation submissions
.doc / .docx — Microsoft Word
  • Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 line spacing, 1-inch margins
  • Sections: Title · Abstract · Introduction · Methods · Results · Discussion · Conclusion · References
  • References: Vancouver style. Tables & figures embedded & numbered
  • No author names/institution in body (blind review). Max 5 MB
e-Poster File
e-Poster competition submissions
.ppt / .pptx — PowerPoint
  • 1 slide only — A0 Portrait: Design → Slide Size → Custom → 33.1" W × 46.8" H
  • Min font: 24pt body, 40pt+ title. High-res images only (≥150 DPI at A0)
  • Include: Title · Authors · Background · Methods · Results · Conclusions · Institution logo
  • Max 20 MB. No embedded videos
CPC Presentation
CPC competition submissions
.ppt / .pptx — PowerPoint
  • Widescreen 16:9, max 20 slides including title
  • Mandatory order: Case History → Investigations → Differentials → Diagnosis → Learning Points
  • Patient fully anonymised. ECG/X-ray/CT allowed if de-identified
  • Do NOT include final diagnosis in file. Max 30 MB
